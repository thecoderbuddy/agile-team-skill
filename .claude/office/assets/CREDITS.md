# Art Credits

## Characters

The agent characters (`office/assets/custom-chars/`, baked into `office/agents.png`)
are the project owner's own chibi illustrations — standing, walk-cycle GIF, seated,
and half-body variants per agent.

## Office environment

The pixel office (`office/tiles.png`, `office/agents.png`) is built from these
asset packs by `build_assets2.py`. Raw packs are downloaded into `office/assets/lpc/`
by `fetch_lpc.sh` (not committed).

## Characters

Composed from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator layer library
(body, head, clothes, hair — walk + sit animations), recolored programmatically:

- Repo: https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
- Per-asset licenses: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0 / GPL 3.0 (see the repo's CREDITS.csv)
- Original LPC art by: Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Johannes Sjölund (wulax),
  Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), bluecarrot16, and many others —
  full per-layer attribution in the generator repo.

The custom-drawn sprites (door, rug) and all code in this repo are not derived
from the packs above.

Derived images (`tiles.png`, `agents.png`) are redistributed under CC-BY-SA 4.0
to satisfy the share-alike terms of the source packs.
